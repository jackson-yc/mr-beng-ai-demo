    THE OPERATING MODEL

    One conversation. Clear ownership.

    A proposed journey for a used-car dealership, with people in control.

    01

    Attract & qualify

    From an advert to a relevant shortlist.

    CUSTOMER CHANNEL

    New enquiry

    Customer responds to a sample advert in a WhatsApp-style conversation.

    Source + permission to continue
    ↓
    SALES AGENT

    Understand the need

    Ask about vehicle type, budget and timing. Suggest a shortlist from approved stock data.

    Preferences + vehicle interest
    Missing information?

    Ask one useful question. Never guess affordability, availability or price.

    02

    Prepare & hand over

    Give staff the context, not another blank lead.

    FINANCE ENQUIRY AGENT

    Collect preferences

    If the customer asks about finance, collect their stated budget and preferred term. No eligibility decision.

    Enquiry summary, not a loan offer
    ↓
    HUMAN CHECKPOINT

    Staff verify & respond

    A salesperson checks stock, confirms pricing and arranges finance advice through the authorised process.

    Named staff owner + next action
    “I want to speak to someone.”

    Skip automated qualification and route to a person. If unavailable, queue for follow-up without promising a response time.

    03

    Visit & follow up

    Make the next step visible to everyone.

    SALES TEAM

    Arrange a showroom visit

    Customer and staff agree a slot after checking availability. A booking is not a purchase.

    Confirmed visit + staff owner
    ↓
    FOLLOW-UP AGENT

    Keep the conversation moving

    Prepare an approved reminder or follow-up. Honour channel permission and opt-out rules.

    Follow-up draft + lead status
    Customer opts out?

    Stop automated follow-ups and retain only what the approved retention policy permits.

    ADS AGENT

    Which source created the enquiry?

    Connect campaign attribution to lead stages. Actual ad access and reliable tracking are future integration work.

    →
    OWNER AGENT

    What needs attention?

    Summarise enquiries, pending reviews and visits. A visit is never reported as a sale.

    What this prototype demonstrates

    Clickable departments, a scripted customer journey, staff review gates, exception paths and an illustrative owner summary.

    What a live pilot would require

    Approved inventory/pricing source, channel access, consent rules, staff ownership, booking integration, finance boundaries and an agreed test plan.

    A CLIENT-READY WALKTHROUGH

    Show the experience. Then discuss the fit.

    Use the buttons below to rehearse each part of the conversation.

    THE OPENING

    “What happens to an enquiry when your sales team is busy?”

    This concept shows how a dealership could give each enquiry a clear next step—without removing the salesperson from the relationship.

    Present this as an illustrative prototype, not an existing connected AI service.
    1. 1. Show the dealership

      Introduce the Sales, Finance Enquiry, Ads and Owner agents, with a visible human handoff desk. Select a department to inspect its responsibilities.

    2. 2. Walk one lead through

      Follow Alex’s sample enquiry from an advert to a shortlist, finance questions and staff review. Click “Simulate staff review” before scheduling the demo visit.

    3. 3. Show the safeguards

      Try a missing budget and an explicit request for a person. Explain that the system asks, pauses or hands over rather than inventing an answer.

    4. 4. Close with a practical pilot

      Agree one channel and one sales journey first. Define success measures together—such as accurate answers and complete handoffs—without promising a revenue uplift.

    Questions for the dealership

    EnquiriesWhich channel brings most enquiries? What questions repeat?

    DataWhere do current stock and approved prices live?

    PeopleWho receives handoffs, and when are they available?

    GuardrailsWhich finance and pricing questions must go to a person?
